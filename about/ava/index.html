A few questions before we start.

Can Ava give personalised payment processing advice?

Yes. Tell Ava your business model, country, monthly processing volume, customer locations and platform. She can help compare directory listings and explain trade-offs. This is AI-assisted research, not regulated financial advice or a broker service.

Can Ava help me choose a high-risk merchant account?

Ava can point you toward specialist providers and help identify eligibility questions, reserve terms and documentation to discuss. Only the provider can assess your application and decide whether to approve it.

Can I use Ava as a Stripe alternatives advisor?

Yes. Explain what happened and what your business needs. Ava can suggest alternatives worth researching, including dedicated merchant accounts, gateways and merchant-of-record providers. A previous decline does not guarantee approval elsewhere.

Is Ava a real personal payment consultant?

No. Ava is PSPDex’s fictional AI payments advisor, not a human consultant. Her lifestyle, quotes and images are creative brand storytelling. She has no real employment history, qualifications or client testimonials.

Does a consultation cost anything or guarantee better rates?

There is no consultation payment step in the Ava chat. Ava cannot negotiate rates, open an account or guarantee savings, approval or settlement terms. Confirm all commercial details directly with a provider before deciding.
