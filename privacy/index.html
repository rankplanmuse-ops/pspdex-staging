Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information PSPDex handles and why.

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Information you provide

When you request pricing, PSPDex records the details you submit, which may include your business name, work email, industry, monthly processing volume, country, and any notes you add.

How information is used

  • To receive, review, and facilitate the enquiry you requested.
  • To contact you about that enquiry.
  • To maintain the security, reliability, and performance of the site.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and prevent misuse.

When information may be shared

Quote-request information may be shared with the processor you selected, or with a relevant provider when needed to facilitate your requested introduction. PSPDex may also use service providers that support hosting, data storage, and site operations. Information may be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect the site and its users.

Site data and external links

Basic technical information may be processed to operate and secure the site. PSPDex links to third-party websites, which apply their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for how those sites collect or use information.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for the purposes described above and applicable obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit a quote request. Requests concerning submitted information can be raised through the same enquiry channel used on the relevant processor page. Identity verification may be required before fulfilling a request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site and its practices change. The date above indicates the current version.
